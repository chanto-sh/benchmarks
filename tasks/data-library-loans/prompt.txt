The board meeting is next week and they asked for some loan stats from library.db (SQLite). The standard loan period is stored in the settings table. Please put the answers in answers.json with these keys:

- "overdue_as_of_2026_09_30": how many loans were overdue at the end of 2026-09-30, i.e. still checked out that day and past their due date.
- "member_most_late_returns": the name of the member who returned the most loans late.
- "avg_history_loan_days": the average number of days a History book was out, over loans that have been returned, rounded to 2 decimals.
- "loans_in_2024": how many loans were made in calendar year 2024.

If the database can't answer one of these, write "not in data" for it instead of a number.
