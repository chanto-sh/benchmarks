real_roots() in roots.py is missing roots. Users report that fits with a repeated root (like (x-2)^2 (x+1)) only show one root or none, two roots very close together come back as nothing, and a root sitting exactly on the interval boundary sometimes disappears. It's supposed to return every distinct real root in [lo, hi] (inclusive), sorted, each accurate to 1e-9, for integer-coefficient polynomials up to degree ~15 with coefficients that can be large. lo and hi are ints or fractions.Fraction. Please make it reliable rather than just bumping the grid resolution; test_roots.py has the basic cases.
