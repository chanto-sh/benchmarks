Finance wants a few numbers from the order export in this folder (see README.txt for what the files are) before the quarterly review. Please write them to answers.json with exactly these keys:

- "q2_revenue_usd": total revenue in USD from completed orders placed in Q2 2026 (April through June), converting non-USD orders with that month's rate from fx.csv. Revenue is quantity times the price the customer paid. Rows with no quantity are broken records; leave them out. Round to 2 decimals.
- "top_region_by_customers": the region (lowercase) with the most distinct customers across all completed orders.
- "duplicate_rows_removed": how many duplicate rows you dropped.
- "q2_gross_margin_pct": the gross margin percentage for Q2 completed orders, rounded to 2 decimals.

If something can't be answered from these files, put the string "not in data" for that key rather than estimating.
