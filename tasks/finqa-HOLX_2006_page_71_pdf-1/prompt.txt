Answer the financial questions in `questions.json`. Each item has a question, a table from a company filing and the surrounding text. Compute the answer from the table (the filing text explains units and definitions) and write `answers.json`, an object mapping each id to its final answer. Answers are compared numerically, so `14`, `14.0`, and `14%` are all accepted for a percentage; yes/no answers are compared case-insensitively.
