Our range-table generator needs a proper projectile model with air resistance. Please implement `flight(v0, angle_deg, mass, k, g=9.81, h0=0.0)` in projectile.py: launch from height h0 (metres, >= 0) at speed v0 (m/s) and elevation angle_deg, with quadratic drag force F = -k |v| v (k in kg/m), and return a dict with "range" (horizontal distance where it comes back down to y = 0), "apex" (maximum height above y = 0) and "time" (time of flight). There's no closed form with drag so it has to be integrated numerically, but the table is printed to four significant figures, so the numbers need to be accurate to better than 0.01% across the whole table, not just for the example in test_projectile.py.
