Implement `p_at_least(r, b, g, k, m)` in odds.py: the probability that at least k red cards were turned up before the dealer stopped, as a fractions.Fraction. Cards are not put back. Sizes go up to about 40 of each colour. There's a test file with a couple of sanity cases.
